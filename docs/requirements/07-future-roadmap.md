# SheoMart - Product Roadmap

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

This document defines the planned evolution of SheoMart.

The roadmap provides a phased development strategy to ensure stable growth while maintaining scalability, security, and maintainability.

Features are organized by product versions rather than implementation order.

---

# Product Vision

SheoMart aims to become a scalable digital marketplace connecting local grocery stores with customers while empowering small businesses through modern inventory management, secure commerce, and data-driven insights.

The initial launch focuses on Sheopur district, with future expansion designed into the architecture from day one.

# Long-Term Vision

SheoMart aims to become a comprehensive platform for local retail businesses by providing:

- Secure commerce
- Inventory automation
- Customer relationship management
- Business analytics
- Financial insights
- AI-assisted operations

The architecture shall remain flexible to support future technologies without requiring major redesign.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every future feature should satisfy the following:

- Improve customer experience.
- Improve store owner productivity.
- Maintain strong security.
- Preserve data integrity.
- Be scalable.
- Be maintainable.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# Out of Scope

The following are intentionally excluded from the initial versions:

- National marketplace
- International expansion
- Cryptocurrency payments
- Social networking
- Live video shopping
- Subscription grocery boxes

These may be evaluated only after the platform reaches product maturity.
