# SheoMart - User Stories

Version: v1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

This document defines the user stories for SheoMart.

User stories describe the system from the perspective of end users and help prioritize feature development.

Each story follows the format:

> As a <role>, I want <feature>, so that <benefit>.

---

# 2. Customer User Stories

## Account

US-C001

As a customer, I want to create an account so that I can place grocery orders.

---

US-C002

As a customer, I want to securely log in so that my personal information and orders remain protected.

---

US-C003

As a customer, I want to reset my password if I forget it.

---

US-C004

As a customer, I want to update my profile information.

---

## Store Discovery

US-C005

As a customer, I want to view available grocery stores in my district.

---

US-C006

As a customer, I want to view store details before shopping.

---

## Products

US-C007

As a customer, I want to browse products by category.

---

US-C008

As a customer, I want to search for products quickly.

---

US-C009

As a customer, I want to see product images, prices, and stock availability.

## Shopping Cart

US-C010

As a customer, I want to add products to my cart.

---

US-C011

As a customer, I want to change product quantities.

---

US-C012

---

US-C013

## Orders

As a customer, I want to place an order.

---

US-C015

As a customer, I want to track my order status.

---

As a customer, I want to cancel an order before it is prepared.

---

US-C017

As a customer, I want to view my previous orders.

---

US-C018

As a customer, I want to pay online when required.

## Notifications

US-C020

# 6. User Story Priorities

## Must Have (v1.0)

- Authentication
- Store Registration
- Product Management
- Categories
- Shopping Cart
- Orders
- Customer Verification
- Admin Dashboard
- Basic Analytics

---

## Should Have (v1.1)

- Push Notifications
- Online Payments
- Better Search
- Product Offers

---

## Could Have (v2.0)

- Delivery Partners
- Loyalty Program
- Coupons
- Reviews
- Wishlist
- QR Store Access
- AI Recommendations

---

## Won't Have (v1.0)

The following are intentionally excluded from the first release:

- Multi-language Support
- Multi-country Support
- Live Chat
- Voice Ordering
- Subscription Orders
- Inventory Forecasting
